# The product is called Limeghost — August 31, 2026

Clearframe is renamed **Limeghost**. This records why, and what was checked, so
the decision does not get re-opened from memory in six months.

## Why Clearframe had to go

- **`clearframe.com` is an operating software company.** Not parked, not for
  sale: a live media-preservation product ("Media preservation pipeline · Cinema
  Blue") with a real sign-in. The domain is unobtainable.
- `clearframe` is taken on **.com, .net, .io, .ai and .co** — the whole family,
  which is what a single holder looks like.
- That company is also software, so a trademark filing in classes 9 and 42 would
  have been made against an existing user of the identical name.

An earlier note in this repository claimed the name was clear. It was not
checked; it was assumed.

## Why Limeghost

**The barn owl is the ghost.** *Tyto alba* is called the **ghost owl** across
England, America and much of Europe — the pale heart-shaped face, the silent
flight, the way it appears white out of the dark. The mark drawn on August 31
is a barn owl's facial disc. So the name is not decoration attached to the
logo; it is a description of it.

**Lime is the accent.** `ClearframeTheme.accent` is `#66DB7D`. The name carries
the colour the product already uses.

**The name does the differentiating.** This is the rule that survived the
search: name and mark must cover different ground. Every name containing *owl*
or *bird* collided with something. Limeghost has a bird logo and a name that
never says bird — so it walks into neither Duolingo's nickname nor Owl
Browser's category.

## What was checked

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| `.com` `.net` `.io` `.ai` `.co` | All five available |
| Any company using the name | **None.** Only personal handles — a Neocities page, a Twitch emote, a YouTube channel with 145 subscribers, a Tumblr, a Letterboxd account |
| Category | Four small browsers are called *Ghost* (two iOS, one Android, one Windows). Distinct as a compound; accepted as a known risk |

Around 140 names were checked across bird, owl, clarity, frame and mask
families. Exactly three had a free `.com`: `birdframes`, `ofbird`, `limeghost`.

## What was rejected, and why — do not re-propose without new evidence

- **Owl names** — `owlbrowser.net` is an existing AI-assisted, privacy-focused
  browser by Olib AI, described in its own words as "for people who want fast,
  privacy-first browsing with native AI tools." That is this product's
  positioning, under an owl name. Also *Owl Browser* on Google Play, plus Owl
  Labs, Hootsuite, and OWL as the W3C Web Ontology Language.
- **Ghost alone** — four shipping browsers already carry it, and `ghost.org` is
  a major publishing platform. The compound is what makes Limeghost viable.
- **Green owl** — "the green owl app" is Duolingo's colloquial name. Searching
  it returns Duolingo discussions. A green owl icon sits in their shadow
  regardless of the name; the drawings differ, but the combination is theirs.
- **Ofbird** — free everywhere and no trademark anywhere, but "of" is a
  preposition, "of bird" is not English, and the `f-b` cluster stumbles. It was
  found in an availability search rather than designed, and it sounds like it.
- **Birdframes** — free everywhere; plural, and two nouns bolted together that
  resolve into "eyeglasses" rather than a browser.
- **Mask names** — MetaMask casts a large shadow inside the browser world
  specifically, `#limemask` already means lime skincare, and `limemask` doubles
  an M so it blurs when spoken.
- **Zincoo** — the founder's own studio, and `docs/ip-and-ownership.md` line 103
  deliberately carves the Zincoo brand *out* of what transfers in a sale. Naming
  the product Zincoo would collapse that separation and merge an agency
  identity with a consumer product. Ship from `zincoo.com/limeghost` instead,
  which is what that document already plans.

## Still open

- **Trademark clearance is a screening result, not legal advice.** WIPO's
  Global Brand Database returned no results for `ofbird`; Limeghost has not had
  the same 89-registry search run against it. Do that before filing, and use an
  attorney.
- **Handles.** `@limeghost` is taken on YouTube, Tumblr and Letterboxd by
  dormant personal accounts. `@limeghostapp` or similar will be needed.
- **The bundle identifier stays `com.clearframe.browser` for now.** Changing it
  orphans every bookmark, history entry, profile and preference on the founder's
  own machine — profile data lives in `com.clearframe.browser.profile.<uuid>`.
  It is invisible to users, so there is no hurry; migrating it needs code that
  copies the old data forward first.
